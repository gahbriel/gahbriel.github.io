Privacy policy

Meridian is a personal app used only by its owner, on the owner's own laptop.

  • When the owner signs in with Google, Meridian reads the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts, and writes calendar events and mail only when the owner asks it to.
  • Everything it reads is kept on the owner's laptop. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • Google user data is used only to give the owner these features, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • The owner can remove Meridian's access at any time from their Google Account (Security, Third-party access).
